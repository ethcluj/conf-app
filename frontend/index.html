VEnue

The UTCN Hub is a brand-new space at the Technical University of Cluj-Napoca, dedicated to advanced scientific research and development. Its purpose is to encourage and support innovation, as well as to foster educational initiatives that elevate the local technological landscape.

Travel Tips

Cluj-Napoca, often dubbed the "Silicon Valley of Eastern Europe", is a vibrant, student-driven, and tech-friendly city nestled in the historical region of Transylvania. Cluj-Napoca (or simply Cluj) blends medieval charm with a youthful pulse. Ranked as one of the safest cities in Europe, it offers a comfortable and welcoming environment. Locals are friendly, and most young people speak English well, making it easy to get around and connect.

We have compiled a short and useful travel guide to help plan your trip and get around. If you have any questions, we suggest joining our telegram group.
